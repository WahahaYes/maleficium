\begin{problem}[5 points]
Show that $\sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$ for every $n \ge 1$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
By induction on $n$. The case $n = 1$ holds. If it holds for $n$, then
\[ \sum_{k=1}^{n+1} k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2} + (n+1) = \frac{(n+1)(n+2)}{2}. \qedhere \]
\end{solution}
